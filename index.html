About

I'm an Information Technology student at Universitas Sumatera Utara (GPA 3.85 / 4.00) focused on application security. I find and validate vulnerabilities through penetration testing projects, Capture the Flag competitions, and security labs, using tools like Burp Suite, Wireshark, Ghidra, GDB, Python, and Linux.

I also build what I test. As the backend engineer of the ITLG Lab Management System, I secured its APIs with JWT authentication and role-based access control, which taught me to look at an application from both sides: as the attacker searching for a way in, and as the developer who wrote it.

I'm certified in Google Cybersecurity and Cisco Ethical Hacker. Outside of pentesting I teach, as a laboratory teaching assistant and a coding instructor, and recently gave a talk on reverse engineering and binary analysis at GDG on Campus USU. If you'd like to work together, my inbox is open at imanuelmtm@gmail.com.
