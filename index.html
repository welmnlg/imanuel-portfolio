About

I'm an Information Technology student with a growing passion for building secure, thoughtful, and well-crafted applications. My long-term goal is to become a security consultant, and I believe that understanding security starts with understanding how things are built. That's why I'm eager to explore app development from the developer's perspective, strengthening both sides of my understanding.

I've had a small taste of that mindset through projects like the ITLG Lab Management System, where I worked on the backend architecture and implemented authentication and access control. That experience showed me how closely good development and good security go hand in hand.

I learn best by doing, asking questions, and working closely with others. I'm genuinely excited to keep growing as a developer, contribute to the community, and stay curious about what I can learn next.
